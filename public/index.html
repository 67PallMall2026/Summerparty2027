The third Melbourne Summer Party

Long afternoons on the banks of the Yarra, with Champagne in hand and the barbecue going

The 67 Pall Mall Summer Party returns to Melbourne for its third year. After last year’s beach setting, we turn to another of the city’s great summer pastimes: long afternoons on the river.

For more than 160 years, Melburnians have made their way to Studley Park Boathouse to while away summer afternoons by the water. On Saturday 6 February, 67 Pall Mall adds its own chapter to that tradition, taking our global Summer Party from afternoon through twilight and into the night, with fine wine, good food and the company of our growing Melbourne Member community.

Summer Parties are a global Member celebration, a chance to reconnect with familiar faces and welcome new ones. This year, for the first time, ticket allocations are uncapped: until we reach capacity, Members are welcome to share the party with their wider networks.

The setting

Among the trees at the historic boathouse

Expect a distinctly Australian summer evening: Zaltos in hand, Champagne flowing, boats drifting along the Yarra and the sizzle of the barbecue carrying across the riverbank. As afternoon slips into evening, the music turns up and the party follows suit.

Mini wine walkaround
